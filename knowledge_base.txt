International Travel: Every international business trip must be pre-approved by your line manager and then by the Finance Director at least 21 days before departure. Submit the request in the TravelDesk portal, which issues a trip reference number. Book economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more, and business class needs written approval from a Vice President. Register the trip with the company travel-safety service and check the official government travel advisory for your destination 48 hours before you leave. Hotels are reimbursed up to 200 USD per night, and a daily allowance of 75 USD covers meals and incidentals. Travel insurance is included automatically for trips booked through TravelDesk.

Domestic Travel: Domestic business trips need approval from your line manager only and must be booked through TravelDesk at least 7 days in advance. Economy class is mandatory for all domestic flights, and rail is preferred for journeys under 4 hours. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at 0.45 USD per kilometre. Hotels are capped at 140 USD per night, and a meal allowance of 40 USD per day applies only when the trip includes an overnight stay.

Expense Reimbursement: Submit expense claims in the ClaimIt app within 30 days of the expense date; claims older than 60 days are not reimbursed. Attach an itemised receipt for every item over 25 USD. Travel expense claims must include the TravelDesk trip reference number. Each claim is approved first by your line manager and then checked by the Finance team. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll, except claims over 1,000 USD, which are paid within 10 working days. Alcohol, traffic fines and personal entertainment are never reimbursed.

Annual Leave: Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year in addition to public holidays. After 5 years of continuous service the entitlement rises to 28 days. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours. Request leave in the HR portal; every request needs manager approval, and requests for more than 10 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year and must be taken by 31 March; any other unused leave is forfeited.

Sick Leave: If you are ill, notify your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. You may self-certify absences of up to 3 consecutive days; from the fourth day a doctor's note is required and must be uploaded to the HR portal. Employees receive up to 15 days of sick leave on full pay per calendar year, followed by up to 30 further days at 50 percent pay. After any absence longer than 5 working days, your manager will hold a return-to-work meeting with you.

Remote Work: With your manager's agreement you may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are anchor days on which every team works from the office. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 local time. A one-off home-office allowance of 300 USD for a desk or chair can be claimed through the expense process within your first 3 months. Working from another country for more than 10 working days in any 12-month period requires prior approval from HR and Legal because of tax and immigration rules.

IT Security: Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, and multi-factor authentication is required on all company accounts. Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Never store company data on personal USB drives or personal cloud storage. Always connect through the company VPN when using public Wi-Fi. Report suspected phishing emails or a lost or stolen device to the IT Security helpdesk on extension 4444 within 1 hour. Security awareness training is mandatory every 12 months.

Company Equipment: New employees receive a company laptop and mobile phone on their first day, and devices are replaced every 3 years. Report damage to or loss of any device to the IT service desk within 24 hours. Installing software that is not in the approved catalogue requires an IT service desk ticket and approval. All equipment must be returned within 5 working days of your last day of employment.

Learning and Development: Each employee has an annual training budget of 1,500 USD and 5 paid study days per year. Courses costing more than 500 USD need manager approval before booking. If you leave the company within 12 months of completing a course that cost more than 2,000 USD, you must repay 50 percent of the course fee.
